Venture finance model

Priced round

SAFEs and the note convert first against today's fully diluted shares; the option-pool top-up is created pre-money; then the round prices at pre-money ÷ pre-round shares.

Cap table

Today, and after conversions and the round. Edit share counts; blue rows are created by the round.

SAFE and note conversion

Post-money SAFEs convert at cap ÷ pre-round shares or the discounted round price, whichever is lower; the note adds simple interest first.

Exit waterfall

Preferences first, pro rata on a shortfall; non-participating preferred converts when that pays more.

Monte Carlo runway

Monthly burn and revenue growth drawn with volatility; seeded so the run is reproducible.
